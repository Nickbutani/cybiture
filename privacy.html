Privacy Policy

Cybiture helps small businesses automate lead follow-up, customer communication, reviews, and online workflows. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, business details, and the message you choose to send. If you book a call, scheduling details may be handled through Cal.com.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to your request, schedule a demo, understand your business needs, provide services, improve our website, and maintain records related to our business relationship.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us run the website, receive form submissions, send email, schedule calls, or deliver our services.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information we receive. No website or online service can guarantee complete security, so please avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information through the contact form.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for business purposes, service delivery, legal requirements, or dispute resolution.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update, delete, or stop using your contact information by emailing us at getsupport@cybiture.com.

Third-Party Links

Our site links to services such as Cal.com. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to getsupport@cybiture.com.
