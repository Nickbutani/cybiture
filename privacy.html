Privacy Policy

Cybiture helps small businesses automate lead follow-up, customer communication, reviews, and online workflows. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use that information, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form, request information, book a call, or otherwise contact us, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, business details, and the message or information you choose to provide.

If you book a call or appointment, scheduling information may be processed through third-party scheduling services such as Cal.com.

How We Use Information

We may use information you provide to respond to your request, communicate with you about your inquiry, schedule demonstrations or appointments, understand your business needs, provide requested services, operate and improve our website, and maintain records related to our business relationship.

Analytics and Website Tracking

We use tools such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use our website, improve website performance, and identify technical or usability issues.

These services may collect information such as device type, browser information, approximate location, pages visited, interactions with the website, and other usage information according to their respective privacy policies.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate our website, process form submissions, send communications, schedule calls, or deliver our services. These providers may use personal information only as necessary to provide services on our behalf.

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent information, and SMS opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Communications

If you provide your mobile phone number and actively consent to receive text messages from Cybiture, we may send SMS messages related to your inquiry, follow-up communications, appointments, and services.

Message frequency varies depending on your interactions with Cybiture. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier and service plan.

You may reply STOP at any time to opt out of receiving SMS messages from Cybiture. You may reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and SMS consent records are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers that assist us with delivering text messages and operating our communication systems. These providers may process mobile information only as necessary to provide services on our behalf.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect the information we receive.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or online service can guarantee complete security. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal, financial, medical, or other confidential information through our general contact forms.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, complying with applicable legal requirements, and resolving disputes.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request that we update, correct, or delete certain contact information we maintain about you, subject to applicable legal and business requirements.

You may contact us at support@cybiture.com .

If you have opted in to SMS communications, you can stop receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may use or link to third-party services, including scheduling, analytics, communications, and other technology providers. Their collection and use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, business practices, or applicable requirements. When we make changes, we may update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at support@cybiture.com .
