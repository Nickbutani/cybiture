# Client Demo Workflow

## The Problem

Most small businesses do not lose leads because people are not interested. They lose leads because follow-up happens too late.

Common leaks:

- Missed calls go to voicemail
- Website forms sit in an inbox
- Quote follow-ups depend on memory
- Review requests are not sent consistently
- Leads are spread across texts, emails, forms, and notes

## The Cybiture Workflow

### 1. Lead Comes In

A customer calls, fills out a form, starts a chat, or requests a quote.

### 2. Lead Gets Captured

The lead is saved into a simple tracker with name, phone, email, service needed, source, and status.

### 3. Owner Gets Alerted

The business owner or team receives an email or SMS alert with the lead details.

### 4. Customer Gets Fast Follow-Up

The customer receives a quick reply by SMS or email.

Example:

"Thanks for reaching out to [Business Name]. We got your request and will follow up shortly. If you want to book now, use this link: [Booking Link]"

### 5. Follow-Up Continues

If the customer does not respond, the system sends 1-2 polite follow-up messages.

### 6. Review Request Goes Out

After the job or appointment is complete, the customer gets a review request.

## What The Client Gets

- Lead capture system
- Lead tracker
- Owner alerts
- Customer auto-reply
- Follow-up messages
- Review request automation
- Monthly system check or report

"Let me show you what happens when a lead comes in. First, the customer submits a form or misses a call. Then the lead is saved here, you get notified, and the customer gets a fast response. This means fewer leads sit around waiting for someone to remember."
