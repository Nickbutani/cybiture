FAQ

Common questions before you automate.

Plain-English answers for small business owners who want faster follow-up without a complicated tech project.

Ask us directlyView services
Do I need to know anything technical?

No. We handle setup, connect the tools, and show you the simple parts you need to use.

How fast can Cybiture be set up?

Most starter workflows can be set up in about 48 hours after we have the right access and details.

Can this work with my current website?

Yes. We can connect forms, booking links, chat, missed-call follow-up, and review requests to most existing small business websites.

What happens after someone fills out my form?

You get the lead details, the customer gets a fast response, and follow-up can continue automatically until they book or reply.

Can I approve messages before they go out?

Yes. For higher-stakes workflows, we can add a review step so you approve messages before they are sent.

Do I need a CRM?

No. We can start with a simple Google Sheet or basic lead dashboard, then connect a CRM later if your business needs one.

Can you help with reviews?

Yes. Review automation can send requests after jobs or appointments and keep the process consistent without manual chasing.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are designed to be simple and month-to-month after setup.
